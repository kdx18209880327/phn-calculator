Feasibility: only six routinely available bedside/laboratory variables are required. Splitting the complete un-split development cohort (n=1789) with the pre-specified outcome-stratified 7:3 procedure (random_state=42) into training (n=1252) and internal-test (n=537), and validating in the external cohort (n=653), gives internal AUC 0.953 and external AUC 0.882, indicating strong, transportable discrimination suitable for risk stratification.

Individual check: the known SHAP force-plot case (Sleep 1 / Scar 1 / Antiviral 0 / NRS 6 / Bili 6.9 / RDW 12.8, original f(x)=0.73) returns 0.73 in this ensemble, confirming faithful reproduction.

Note on admission NRS: in this dataset admission NRS is inversely associated with PHN (higher acute-pain scores correspond to lower predicted risk); it is treated as an associational, non-monotone predictor and interpreted cautiously.

Disclaimer: this is a research/demonstration tool and does not replace clinical judgment. Prospective, multicentre validation and local recalibration are required before clinical use. All computation runs locally in your browser; no data is transmitted.
